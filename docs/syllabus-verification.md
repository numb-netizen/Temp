# HSC 2027 syllabus audit — incomplete / যাচাই অসম্পূর্ণ

## Scope and evidence

Target cohort: students admitted in the 2025–26 academic session. The website's existing checklists are **selected preparation topics**, not verified textbook chapters or a complete examination syllabus. Existing paper labels on those topics also require checking. Do not interpret 100% completion of those topics as finishing a syllabus.

Official source entry points inspected on 2026-11-10:

- [NCTB higher-secondary curriculum](https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/files/6922dbc6933eb65569e0c702) — subject curriculum index; the accessible view did not expose the underlying documents.
- [NCTB 2025–26 higher-secondary textbooks](https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e145933eb65569e2b37b) — the relevant cohort, including বাংলা সাহিত্যপাঠ ও সহপাঠ, English for Today and ICT. The actual book contents could not be read in this environment.
- [NCTB বাংলা প্রথম পত্র notice, 26 November 2025](https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/notices/695b9e5235ce18e1c05af63d) — applies from 2025–26; the notice attachment's chapter list could not be read, so its contents cannot be certified from the title alone.
- [NCTB syllabus and assessment notices](https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dff5933eb65569e24d31) and [Sylhet Board HSC corner](https://sylhetboard.gov.bd/corner/hsc) — consult for subsequent applicable notices. The 2026 exam-specific and 2026–27 admission-cohort documents are not evidence of the 2027 syllabus.
- [Dhaka Board HSC-2026 examiner list](https://dhakaeducationboard.gov.bd/data/20261007113150169280.pdf) shows the historical Bangla first/second paper code conventions; it is **not** an HSC 2027 syllabus and is insufficient to verify every code for this cohort.

The official NCTB pages returned only accessibility-navigation text through the available page reader. Search indexing confirms page titles and some metadata, not full document contents. Secondary book/chapter lists disagree or address other cohorts and were not used as official proof. A locally used college test plan alone would also not establish the full board syllabus.

## Required next steps

1. Obtain readable official 2025–26 curriculum, textbook tables of contents and any applicable education-board notices (including the linked বাংলা first-paper notice). Scan or upload the actual attachment if the government site cannot be read.
2. Record the exact source URL, edition, paper, page, code, and verification date per subject. Resolve any differing lists with the relevant board/college, rather than assuming a 2026 examination list applies.
3. Treat the Bangla second-paper items as image-based selected preparation, not certified chapters; add complete paper-specific chapters only after review. Preserve each existing `hsc2027:<subject>:<chapter>` key and its meaning; do not relabel/reuse a historical key for a different chapter or silently transfer ambiguous progress. Use fresh stable keys for new items, and an explicit migration only where equivalence is proven.
4. Only after all topics and paper assignments are checked, replace the selected-list warnings, implement complete per-paper checklists and accurately scoped progress, and mark the PR ready for review.
